After running the Colab notebook (all cells 1-19), download these two files
from /content/ml_outputs/ in Colab and place them here:

  1. asa_model_*.joblib       → rename to: asa_model.joblib
  2. model_metadata_*.json   → rename to: model_metadata.json

Once both files are here, you can run:
  python ml/predict.py
  uvicorn ml.api.main:app --reload --port 8000

Do NOT commit these files to Git (they are in .gitignore).
